# 💬 About me
- I am a PhD student in the [School of Electronic and Information Engineering](http://eie.hitsz.edu.cn/) at [Harbin Institute of Technology (Shenzhen)](https://www.hitsz.edu.cn/).

- My research focuses on hardware security and device authentication. I study physical unclonable functions (PUFs) and hardware fingerprints based on printed electronics, optical labels, and PCB/RFID structures, with an emphasis on reliable evaluation under real-world measurement conditions.

- I also work on federated IoT intrusion detection and fingerprinting in AI systems.
